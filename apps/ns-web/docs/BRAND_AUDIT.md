## What this means for NATYSIMO

1. **Morocco is the primary market.** Prices are in DH, delivery to Morocco comes first, and cash on
   delivery is planned (checkout asks for a phone number). The design stays international.
2. **Core customer: men aged 18–34.** The masculine, athletic, dark direction is right. Clothing is
   the entry point for the 19 % women; the catalog stays unisex-capable.
3. **Reels are the engine and non-followers are the audience.** Most visitors arrive cold, on a
   phone, from a reel. The first screen has to explain the brand without reading. Hence the cinematic
   hero, the mark first, and three words.
4. **The funnel leaks at the bio link.** 113k profile visits turned into 548 link taps. That is the
   biggest commercial lever, so there is now a dedicated link-in-bio page at `/ig` (one screen, thumb
   targets, the founder's look, the three worlds). **Action: put `natysimo.com/ig` in the bio.**
5. **Saves and shares are high on the top reels.** The content is useful (training, discipline). The
   brand voice is short, direct and motivational, not luxury-cold. The copy follows that.
6. **Founder DNA.** Moroccan roots, based in Germany, training content, a paid training plan, and
   fast organic growth. The story is told from those facts only. Nothing is invented about
   hardships, dates or numbers.
